Frequently Asked Questions

  • The 3D model isn't displaying correctly
    Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, ensure your device is running the latest OS version and restart your device.
  • Audio isn't playing
    Check that your device isn't on silent mode and that the volume is turned up. The app uses your device's built-in audio output.
  • Can I use this offline?
    Yes. Sanctum Vox Articulator works entirely offline — no internet connection is needed.
  • How do I switch between accents?
    Tap the "Accents" tab in the main panel. Select any of the 10 available accents to view its Wells Lexical Sets and key features.
  • Will more accents be added?
    We're actively developing additional accent modules. If you'd like to request a specific accent, email us at hello@sanctumvox.com.
  • I'd like a refund
    Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.
